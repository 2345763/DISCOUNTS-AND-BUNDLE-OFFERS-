FAQ

Straight answers before you book.

Which prices are monthly and which are one-time?

Offers 01 and 02 are monthly, billed upfront each month for a minimum term. Offer 01: ₹88,980 a month × 6 months = ₹5,33,880 + GST. Offer 02 is valid only on a 12-month contract: ₹6,00,000 + GST. Offer 03, the 3D website, is a one-time fee: ₹80,000 + GST, paid in full upfront, with no monthly contract.

How long are these offers valid?

They're limited-time offers, valid until terminated. There's no fixed end date, but any offer can be withdrawn at any time without notice. Your price is secured once your booking is confirmed, and then stays fixed for your minimum term.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. Offer 01 is available worldwide at $1,200 a month, fully remote. Our team guides you through each and every step remotely, so travel isn't needed; if transportation is ever required, it's billed separately. Offer 02 is for clients in India only.

Are travel and transportation included?

No. Transportation, travel and all other expenses are billed separately where needed. Offer 02 includes two shoot days.

Where is my data stored in the gifted web apps?

Locally, on your own device, locked with your own ID and password, so your business data stays private.

What does “valid until terminated” mean for apps and web apps?

Apps and web apps we deliver, including gifted web apps, have no expiry date. They stay valid, live and supported for as long as your agreement runs, and validity ends only when the agreement is terminated. Hosting, domain and store costs are separate.

Can I pay weekly or in parts?

No. Each month is paid 100% upfront at the start of the billing month. Weekly, partial or split payments are not accepted.

Can I get a refund if I change my mind?

No. All payments are 100% non-refundable, whether before or after work starts, including on early termination. Please read the No-Refund Policy before booking.

Is ad spend included?

No. Ad spend is paid by you directly to the platforms. The same applies to travel, domains, hosting, API usage and other third-party costs.

Do you guarantee followers, leads or sales?

No. Results depend on platforms, your market and budgets, and an agency that promises fixed numbers isn't being straight with you. We commit to the deliverables and the process, and we report honestly every month.

Can an offer be customised or negotiated?

No. Offers are fixed, untouched packages: non-customisable, non-changeable and highly non-negotiable. If you need anything different, smaller or bigger, choose a custom quotation. It's priced 100% to your requirements and it's our most recommended option.

How do coupons work?

CUSQO15, CHECKLIST1L, APP23 and WEB27 are sent to us on WhatsApp; we check eligibility and confirm them. One coupon per booking. Every coupon is valid until terminated and can be terminated at any time, so use yours now.

Will there be more offers?

Yes, more offers are on the way. Tap “Notify me on WhatsApp” on the excitement board to ask us to keep you posted.
